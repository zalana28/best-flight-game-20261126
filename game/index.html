GULLWING COURIER

Island air taxi — three sorties, one little plane, no room for error.

The islands need a courier. Fly the ring run over the valley, put her down on the North Field, then drop the crate on the lake field and come home. Mind your fuel, mind your speed — below 50 knots the wing stops flying.

  • W / S — throttle up / down
  • Arrow keys — pitch and bank (bank to turn)
  • Q / E — rudder
  • Hold left mouse — steer with the mouse
  • Space — drop the crate (sortie 3)
  • C — chase / cockpit camera  ·  P — pause  ·  M — sound

A complete flight game: throttle, lift and stall, fuel and damage, three missions, landings that count. Built for the make.fun best flight game bounty.
